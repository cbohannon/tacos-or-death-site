Privacy Policy

Tacos or Death — Last updated: April 2026

Tacos or Death is a single-player offline game. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data stored locally on your device

  • High score (deliveries completed, money earned)
  • Game settings (volume, resolution, window mode)

This data is saved to your local device only, in the standard application data folder provided by the operating system. It is never sent anywhere.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or login required
  • No online features or multiplayer
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising
  • No third-party SDKs

Steam

Tacos or Death is distributed via Steam. Steam may collect its own usage data subject to the Steam Privacy Policy .
